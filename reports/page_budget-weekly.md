## For the owner to decide (B-12)

- One volume of 3,197 pages at this trim size is far thicker than a usual single-volume breviary; each volume of the four-volume Liturgia Horarum is of the order of 1,500–2,000 pages on bible paper. Without the readings the main volume would have 2,097 pages.
- Options above can be combined; the alleluia rule changes the text as printed (a rubric replaces printed variants) and needs a decision on how the rule is worded.
