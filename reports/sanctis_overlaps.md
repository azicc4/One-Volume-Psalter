# Saints printed in more than one Sanctis volume

Generated by `python -m lh verify` (lh/verify/sanctis_overlaps.py). SPA, SP, SQ, SADV from the database (day keys SANCT.MM-DD.<litcal_key>, lh/sources/sanctis.py).

Each overlapping day resolves to one day key; each printing stays its own set of objects (seasonal forms). Slots are compared on the main printings (not ALTERA):

- **same** — identical object;
- **alleluia** — identical apart from the Easter-time *allelúia* (incl. the Easter shape of short responsories) — a seasonal variant;
- **common** — different texts that are each used on several days of their own volume, i.e. the season's Common or Ordinary (Lenten vs Easter Compline, Easter Common of martyrs …) — expected;
- **variant** — near-identical texts (possible typo) — *review*;
- **differs** — a different proper text — *review*;
- *only A / only B* — slots printed in one volume only (a commemoration prints no hymns or psalmody; memorials print psalmody «de feria»).

A day's texts *agree* when nothing is left to review.
