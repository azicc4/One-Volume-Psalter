Ribbons (top bar)

  • A ribbon lies between two pages, shown as 684 | 685.
  • Click a ribbon to turn to its pages. It stays there while you read elsewhere.
  • Click it again while you are on its pages to take it out (loose).
  • Click a loose ribbon to lay it in the pages you are on.
  • The ribbons are assigned by use: 4–5 keep your places in the Proper of Time (the hour, the week's texts), 7–8 the psalms and canticles of the hour, 3 Compline and Daytime Prayer in the Ordinary.
  • The fixed parts of the hours are on the Ordinary card, resting at the right edge: click it (or press C) to take it out, click it again to turn it over, and click the outline "return card to side" (or Esc) to put it back.
  • Shift-click a page reference in the book (e.g. P 1673, 1702 in an hour heading) to lay the matching ribbon there: P → ribbon 7 for the first psalm, 8 for a canticle or the next psalm; R → Readings (9); O → Ordinary (3). Other references go to the ribbon of the section they point into.
